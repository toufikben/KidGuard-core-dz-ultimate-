cut out of each POST line for readability. Nothing here was written by our scripts.

--- how to read this ---
1. Six accepted POSTs 10:58:22..11:00:49 = school-exit (offsets 0/30/60/90/120/150 s at speed 1);
   four 11:04:17..11:07:16 = route-park; six 11:14:39..11:17:09 = the idempotence re-run. The
   coordinates above match lab/scenarios/school-exit.json and route-park.json reading for reading,
   and the same instants appear as forwards in the sink artifact.
2. The only 400s are for id=lab-not-registered, each preceded by the servers own "Unknown device"
   warning, from loopback AND from the LAN address 10.220.50.85. An unregistered uniqueId cannot
   inject a position: the spec 9.4 whitelist measured server-side, not just asserted by our check
   script.
3. 8082 is bound to 127.0.0.1 only; 5055 is bound to 0.0.0.0 because a phone on the LAN has to reach
   it. Ledger-flagged for pre-production: a public deployment must not publish the ingest port bare.
